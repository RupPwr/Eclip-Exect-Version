<p>Eclip is a fast, powerful video editor for Android designed for simplicity, precision, and privacy. Edit, enhance, and export watermark-free videos entirely on your device.</p>

<p><b>Features</b></p>

<ul>
<li><b>Trim &amp; Split:</b> Precision video cutting, segment rearranging, and frame-accurate timeline editing.</li>
<li><b>Multi-Track Overlays:</b> Add and reorder text, stickers, images, GIFs, and PIP video overlays.</li>
<li><b>Subtitles:</b> Import standard .srt subtitle files with interactive positioning and sizing.</li>
<li><b>Audio Studio:</b> Multi-track audio mixing, 200% volume boosting, and live voiceover recording.</li>
<li><b>Crop &amp; Aspect Ratios:</b> Crop videos to standard 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, or custom aspect ratios.</li>
<li><b>Transitions &amp; Effects:</b> Real-time transition preview animations and visual color adjustments.</li>
<li><b>Speed &amp; Motion:</b> Fast motion, smooth slow motion, freeze frames, and reverse video playback.</li>
<li><b>Hardware Acceleration:</b> Lightning-fast export speeds powered by MediaCodec hardware encoding.</li>
</ul>
